# 🎙️ DevVoice Scratchpad

**DevVoice Scratchpad** is an intelligent, voice-first "Second Brain" designed specifically for software engineers. It captures your raw thoughts, bug reports, and architectural ideas via voice, structures them using AI, and syncs them across all your devices and local storage.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🧠 Project Brain (Permanent Context)
Provide the AI with your project's README, tech stack rules, or coding standards. The AI will prioritize this information whenever you dictate a new note, ensuring all suggestions follow your project's specific "Master Rules."

### 🌐 Global & Project Digests
- **Project Master Digest**: A structured JSON-based report including summaries, technical modules status, and automated Mermaid diagrams of your progress.
- **Global Daily Digest**: A master report aggregating activity from ALL projects, providing a bird's-eye view of your entire day.

### 🎙️ Advanced Voice Input
- **Language Identification (LID)**: Automatically detects the language you speak (EN, UA, PL, DE) and responds in the same language.
- **Caret Insertion**: Dictate and insert text exactly where your cursor is placed in the editor.
- **Mic Island**: A minimalist, floating interface that expands into a powerful Command Center.

### 📂 Universal Synchronization
- **Local-First**: Notes are saved directly to your physical file system as Markdown files.
- **Cloud Sync**: Two-way synchronization with **Firebase Cloud** and **Google Drive**.
- **Bidirectional GDrive Sync**: Notes from the cloud are physically written to your local project folders.

### 🛡️ Security & Encryption
- **Client-Side Encryption (AES-256)**: Your notes are encrypted directly in the browser before being sent to the cloud.
- **Master Password**: Set a password in Settings to protect your note content in Firebase and Google Drive. Without this password, cloud data is unreadable.
- **Privacy First**: We have no access to your passwords or encrypted data. Everything stays under your total control.
- **Local-Clear / Cloud-Encrypted**: Data on your disk remains in clear text for fast local searching, while cloud data is securely protected.

### 🪄 AI Intelligence
- **Magic Fix**: Re-process any note with AI to improve grammar, add markdown structure, or change the note type.
- **Dev Insights**: Real-time technical observations and witty technical debt analysis based on your recent activity.
- **Smart Actions**: Automatically draft GitHub Issues or Architecture Decision Records (ADR) from your raw notes.

### 🎨 Intelligent UI
- **Auto-Categorization**: Notes are colored by type (Bug, Idea, Architecture, Todo, Generic).
- **Smart Views**: Instantly filter notes by category or search across all projects.
- **Zen Mode**: A clean, distraction-free environment for deep work.

## 🌍 Multi-language Support
Full interface and AI processing support for: **English, Ukrainian, Polish, and German**.
